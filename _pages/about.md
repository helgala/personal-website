**Associate Professor, Electrical and Computer Engineering**

**Associate Director, AI & Society College**

I am an associate professor in the [Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering](https://www.albany.edu/ece) at the University at Albany and director of the [Signals and Networks (SINE) Lab](https://www.albany.edu/ece/signals-and-networks-lab). My work spans optical wireless communications, LiFi and visible light communications, AI for communications and networks, hybrid RF–optical systems, wireless sensing, and physical-layer security. I also serve as associate director of the [AI & Society College](https://www.albany.edu/ai-plus/ai-society-college).

Before joining UAlbany, I was a research professor at Boston University. I earned my Ph.D. from Constructor University (formerly Jacobs University) in Germany in 2010.
