I am an Associate Professor in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University at Albany and Associate Director of the AI & Society College. I direct the [Signals and Networks (SINE) Lab](https://www.albany.edu/ece/signals-and-networks-lab).

My research spans optical wireless communications, LiFi and visible light communications, AI for communications and networks, hybrid RF–optical systems, wireless sensing, and physical-layer security.

Before joining UAlbany, I was a research professor at Boston University. I earned my Ph.D. from Constructor University (formerly Jacobs University) in Germany in 2010.

My academic work includes publications in optical wireless communications, visible light communications, machine learning for wireless systems, hybrid RF–optical networks, and physical-layer security.
