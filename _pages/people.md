### Doctoral researchers listed as current in the CV

- **Honglan Chen** — Ph.D. student since Fall 2024; research includes optical OFDM modulation identification with deep learning.
- **Saksham Dewan** — Ph.D. student since Fall 2023; research includes multi-task learning and visible light communications.

### Ph.D. alumni

- **Monette Khadr** — Ph.D. 2021; dissertation on augmented communications for massive-MISO VLC channels.
- **Ahmed Hussein** — Ph.D. 2021; dissertation on mixed-carrier communication for sixth-generation networks.

### Selected M.S. and undergraduate mentoring

- Saksham Dewan — M.S. thesis on multi-task learning for hybrid waveforms, 2024.
- Sai Sandeep Kajukuri — M.S. project on signal and modulation classification, 2022.
- Priti Pachpande — M.S. thesis on communications using deep learning techniques, 2019.
- Team ULTRA — undergraduate LiFi capstone project, first place in ECE research at UAlbany Showcase Day 2025.

I welcome inquiries from students interested in optical wireless systems, AI for communications, and hybrid RF–optical networks. [Email me](mailto:helgala@albany.edu) with your interests and background.
