My research explores how communication waveforms, network architectures, and machine learning can improve future wireless systems. I direct the [Signals and Networks (SINE) Lab](https://www.albany.edu/ece/signals-and-networks-lab) at UAlbany.

### Optical wireless communications and LiFi

Visible light communication uses modulated light sources to carry data. My work addresses LiFi networks, optical OFDM, modulation and dimming, and practical high-speed optical links. The SINE Lab's [research overview](https://www.albany.edu/ece/signals-and-networks-lab) describes this work, and my [publication list]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}) includes the peer-reviewed papers.

### AI for communications and networks

I study machine learning for physical-layer design, waveform demodulation, modulation identification, and wireless network decisions. Recent projects include [IBM–UAlbany research on multi-task wireless waveforms](https://www.albany.edu/news-center/news/2026-latest-ibm-spyre-accelerators-power-new-ualbany-ai-research-projects).

### Hybrid RF–optical networks and sensing

Radio and optical technologies can complement each other in dense networks. My research covers coexistence, radio–optical testbeds, wireless backscattering, joint communication and sensing, and spectrum science. [CHRONOS](https://www.albany.edu/ece/cloud-hybrid-rf-optical-network-over-synchronous-links) is one example of this work.

### AI and society

Through the [AI & Society College](https://www.albany.edu/ai-plus/ai-society-college), I contribute to AI education and public-good programs at UAlbany and across SUNY. The [projects page]({{ '/projects/' | relative_url }}) lists related initiatives from my CV.

For a fuller description of research interests, visit my [UAlbany faculty profile](https://www.albany.edu/ece/faculty/hany-elgala).
