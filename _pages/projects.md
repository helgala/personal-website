These selected awarded initiatives come from my July 2026 CV. Project descriptions and roles are summarized here; institutional pages provide additional context where available.

### Active initiatives

**Deep Learning to Coexist: AI-Accelerated Multi-Task Wireless Waveforms** · 2026

Principal Investigator, IBM–UAlbany Center for Emerging Artificial Intelligence Systems. The work explores wireless signals that can support multiple tasks, including communication and sensing. [UAlbany project overview](https://www.albany.edu/news-center/news/2026-latest-ibm-spyre-accelerators-power-new-ualbany-ai-research-projects).

**InSpect: Instrument for Research and Education in Spectrum Science** · 2026

Co-Principal Investigator on an NSF Major Research Instrumentation award establishing an advanced wireless testbed. [UAlbany announcement](https://www.albany.edu/news-center/news/2026-ualbany-receives-13m-nsf-award-establish-advanced-wireless-testbed).

**Advancing AI for the Public Good** · 2026–2028

SUNY AI Partnership/Empire AI initiative supporting AI teaching and research across the region. [AI & Society College initiatives](https://www.albany.edu/ai-plus/ai-society-college).

**AI & Society Commons** · 2026–2028

A SUNY digital knowledge lab for teaching, learning, and critical inquiry, supported by a SUNY Academic Innovation Grant. [About the Commons](https://www.albany.edu/ai-plus/ai-society-college).

### Selected earlier projects

**CHRONOS: Cloud-Based Hybrid RF-Optical Network over Synchronous Links** · 2018–2024

Co-Principal Investigator on an NSF-supported hybrid network research infrastructure project. [Project site](https://www.albany.edu/ece/cloud-hybrid-rf-optical-network-over-synchronous-links).

**Coexistence of Directional Communications within 5G Networks** · 2016–2020

Principal Investigator on an NSF project studying visible-light-enhanced small cells with collaborators at Boston University and New Jersey Institute of Technology.

**FlyServ: Indoor Hybrid Flying Networks for Safety Applications and Services** · 2020–2024

Co-Principal Investigator with Al Ain University and Khalifa University.
