The University at Albany announced a $1.3 million National Science Foundation award to establish an advanced wireless testbed for research and education in spectrum science. Hany Elgala is a co-principal investigator alongside Dola Saha, Aveek Dutta, and Mustafa Aksoy.

The facility will support experiments across a wider range of frequencies and complement the existing [CHRONOS hybrid RF–optical testbed](https://www.albany.edu/ece/cloud-hybrid-rf-optical-network-over-synchronous-links).
