UAlbany announced new research projects using IBM Spyre accelerators through the IBM–UAlbany Center for Emerging Artificial Intelligence Systems. Hany Elgala's project, _Deep Learning to Coexist: Spyre-Accelerated Multi-Task Wireless Waveforms_, investigates signals that can perform more than one task, such as communicating with a drone while sensing its presence.
